Prototype calibration of the product-manager seat, 40 worlds per manager and case.
Reproduce: node proto/venture/run.ts 40 [--from 101] ; node proto/venture/items.ts <0|1> 40
Priors were written into run.ts before the first run. Money is rupees lakh (1 L = 100,000 rupees). The thresholds and constants were tuned while looking at seeds 1-40; seeds 101-140 were not looked at until the end.
Three ranges were REVISED AFTER SEEING RESULTS, at the owner's instruction. debt_first: written 25-45, first run 60-65, weights changed (option D), range now 45-55. random: written 15-35, observed 31.2-36.7, now 30-40. evidence_led: written 65-90, observed 91.6-92.9, now 90-95. The last two were set to fit what was observed, so landing inside them proves nothing about the original expectations. The ranges apply to a mean over 40 games. do_nothing (0-5), textbook (40-60) and feature_factory (20-40) are as first written.
These tables are from the rubric with option D. Seeds 101-140 were seen under the first weights before the weights were changed, so they are not fully held out for that change.
