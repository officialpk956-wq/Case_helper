Privacy and terms

This note explains what pm-sim stores when you use this site. For privacy questions, contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Privacy

  • Account and play data: the server stores your username, a salted scrypt password hash, sign-in session tokens, case decisions, and simulation results on its storage. It does not store the password itself. Your session lasts up to seven days.
  • Browser data: the HttpOnly session cookie keeps you signed in. Browser storage holds drafts, timers, unfinished-run shortcuts, and your theme choice. Clear this site's browser data to remove those local items.
  • Hosting: if this site is public, your requests reach its hosting provider over HTTPS, and the provider may keep operational request logs under its own policy. pm-sim does not add analytics, advertising trackers, third-party fonts, or marketing email.
  • Access: other accounts cannot access your runs through the app. The server operator and hosting provider can access stored data, so do not enter confidential information or reuse a password.
  • Retention and deletion: account data and runs remain until you choose “Delete account”; the app then removes your account, sessions and runs. The oldest runs may be removed earlier when storage limits are reached. Hosting backups or logs may persist under the provider's policy. Browser data must be cleared separately.

Terms

  • pm-sim is a practice tool. Its companies, people, numbers and events are invented for the exercise.
  • A score shows how this simulation judged your decisions in an invented world. It is no qualification, no assessment of your ability and no advice for a real company. The effect sizes behind each case are placeholders that nobody who does these jobs has reviewed.
  • Keep real confidential information and real passwords out of the free-text answers and out of your username.
  • The software is provided as it is, without warranty. Availability and saved progress may be interrupted by hosting problems.
  • Create an account only if you accept these notes. Anyone who changes the software should change this page to match.
